CLV = our odds ÷ Pinnacle's no-vig closing price − 1. Every settled bet carries a CLV, shown as "pending" while the closing price is still being checked. "≈" marks the case where Pinnacle offered no market on that exact line, so the close was derived from its nearest line or from the closing consensus of major US books. Voided bets have no CLV.

Open free picks are shown with their result "OPEN" and do not count in the numbers above until they are settled. Odds are the price at the moment the pick was issued (until 16 Nov: as posted in our Telegram channel). Stakes are in units as issued (1u = 1 % of bankroll). A win pays stake × (odds − 1), a loss costs the stake, pushes and voids pay 0. On quarter lines a bet can be half won or half lost (½): half of that profit or loss on the full stake, counted in the third number. ROI = profit ÷ total staked.
